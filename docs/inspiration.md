## 2026-11-23 · "Have the audacity to be delusional"
- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ahhYA66Vi9w
- **Creator:** BullCharge Forex Automation (sells forex trading robots, so the "system" line is a lead-in to their pitch)
- **Length:** 2:10

### Core ideas
1. The guy who believes he's the luckiest man alive usually is. Belief shapes what happens.
2. The mind is the main battle. How you see yourself matters more than diet, supplements or your body.
3. The chain: **thoughts → beliefs → habits → reality.** The mind works for you or against you. It's never neutral.
4. Believing things are working in your favour won't make life worse.
5. **Belief is only half.** It gives you direction, but you need a **system** that delivers results no matter how you feel.

### Honest read
- Point 5 is the strongest. Belief is the compass and the system is the engine.
- Point 4 overreaches. Blind belief without checks can hurt, especially with money. The honest "Where you are now" step balances it.
- Ignore the product pitch and keep the idea.
